# Discussion Exercises

For each scenario, discuss:

1. What is the request asking you to do?
2. What should you verify, and how?
3. When should you report or escalate it?

These are discussion exercises. Do not send the examples as actual emails.

## Scenario 1: Account alert

You receive a message saying your work account will be disabled in one hour unless you sign in using a link in the message.

The message displays the name of a service your organization uses.

**Discuss:** What would you do before entering any account information?

---

## Scenario 2: Vendor payment change

A familiar vendor emails an updated invoice. The message says the vendor has changed banks and asks you to send this month's payment to the new account.

The sender asks for the change to be processed today.

**Discuss:** How would you verify the bank details?

---

## Scenario 3: Shared document

A message says a coworker shared a document with you. You were not expecting a document, but the message displays your coworker's name.

The linked page asks you to sign in.

**Discuss:** How would you check whether the request is legitimate?

---

## Follow-up question

Suppose you clicked the link or entered information before realizing the message might be suspicious.

**Discuss:** Who should you contact, and what should you tell them?
